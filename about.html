The Legal Clinic is the clinical legal education arm of the Sto. Niño Mactan College – School of Law in Soong, Lapu-Lapu City. We exist for two reasons: to widen access to justice across Mactan, and to train law students to practise competently and ethically. Every client is served by certified law student practitioners working under Rule 138-A, with a supervising attorney who reviews and signs off on all work before it is released.

01

Access to justice

Free legal advice, drafted documents and case assistance for residents of Lapu-Lapu City who cannot afford private counsel.

02

Supervised practice

Level 1 practitioners interview, research and draft. Level 2 practitioners may appear before courts and quasi-judicial bodies within the limits of Rule 138-A.

03

Professional ethics

The clinic follows the Code of Professional Responsibility and Accountability on confidentiality, conflicts of interest, and competence.
