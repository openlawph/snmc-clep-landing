How the SNMC Law Legal Clinic handles the personal information you give us, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

01

Who we are

The SNMC Law Legal Clinic is run by law student practitioners of Sto. Niño Mactan College of Law, under the supervision of lawyers. We are responsible for the personal information you give us through this website.

02

What we collect

When you book a visit online, we ask for:

  • Your name and personal details, such as age, sex, civil status and occupation.
  • Your contact details and address.
  • Household, income and property information, to check if you qualify for free legal help.
  • The facts of your concern, the name of the other party, and whether a lawyer already handles your matter.
  • Your consent and your typed signature.

Some of this may be sensitive personal information. Please give us only what the form asks for.

03

Why we use it

We use your information only to:

  • decide if you qualify for free legal assistance;
  • check for conflicts of interest;
  • contact you about your appointment; and
  • give you legal service and keep our records.

04

Who can see it

Your information is seen only by the clinic staff, law student practitioners and supervising lawyers who work on your request. We do not sell it. We share it outside the clinic only when you ask us to, when it is needed to refer you to another office, or when the law requires it.

05

Confidentiality

All information you provide is treated with strict confidentiality in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and the attorney-client privilege.

06

How it is stored

Your booking is stored on the clinic’s server, and only signed-in clinic staff can open it. The booking form uses a Cloudflare security check to keep out spam.

07

What your browser keeps

This site saves your language choice (English or Bisaya) in your browser so it remembers it next time. You can clear it in your browser settings.

08

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you may:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • dispute and correct anything that is wrong;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to block or delete it, as the law allows; and
  • complain to the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, email legal-clinic@snmclaw.com. If you withdraw your consent, we cannot continue to process your online request.

09

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date below shows when it last changed.
