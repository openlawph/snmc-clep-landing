Data privacy consent (RA 10173)

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), the SNMC Legal Clinic collects, stores, and processes personal and case-related information solely to determine qualification for free legal assistance and for the continuous rendition of legal service. All disclosures are protected under attorney-client privilege. Clients retain full rights to access, dispute inaccuracies, and withdraw consent in accordance with law.

We cannot process an online request without your consent. You can still visit the clinic in person during office hours and our staff will assist you.
