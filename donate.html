The clinic gives its legal services free of charge. What it cannot give away is the cost of getting to people — fares, paper, tarpaulins, a room in a barangay hall. Our Bar Operations drive is separate: it carries our graduates through the Bar examinations. You may give to either, or to both.

Drive 01

Charity and community outreach

This drive keeps the clinic on the road. It pays for the paper and printing behind every affidavit and pleading we release for an indigent client, for legal information sessions in the barangays of Lapu-Lapu City, and for relief-and-legal-help drives after a typhoon, when people lose the documents that prove who they are and what they own.

What a gift covers

  • Printing, photocopying and documentary costs on indigent client files
  • Barangay legal information sessions and mobile legal aid days
  • Fares for clients who must attend a hearing or barangay conciliation

Drive 02

Bar Operations

Bar Ops is how the School of Law sends its graduates into the Bar examinations prepared and looked after. Contributions go to the candidates who cannot carry the cost alone, and to the command centre our students run on examination weekends so that no examinee from this school sits the Bar without someone waiting outside for them.

Where Bar Ops funds go

  • Review materials and review-centre fees for graduates in need
  • Lodging, meals and transport on examination weekends
  • The Bar Ops command centre, first aid and hydration for examinees

How to give

In cash

Over the counter at the College Finance Office, or by bank transfer or e-wallet to the College account. Name the fund you are giving to — Legal Clinic Outreach or Bar Operations — so it is credited correctly.

In kind

Bond paper, folders, printer ink and tarpaulins for outreach; food, water and medical supplies for Bar Ops. Please coordinate with the Clinic Coordinator before delivering anything.

Volunteer

Alumni lawyers, notaries public and student volunteers are welcome on legal aid days and on Bar Ops shifts. Email the clinic with the dates you are free and what you can do.

The clinic charges no fee for legal services at any stage. Donations are received by Sto. Niño Mactan College and acknowledged by the Finance Office; they are not payment for a case and they have no bearing on how any case is handled. No law student practitioner may solicit or accept anything of value from a client. If anyone asks you for money in the clinic's name, report it to the Clinical Program Director.
